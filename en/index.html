Why Choose Us

Factory Strength Drives Supply Stability

Full process control from raw material to finished yarn makes price, quality and lead time more predictable.

Own factory, direct supply

Spinning, texturing and twisting are all done in-house, removing middlemen so price and lead time stay under control.

Batch-stable quality

Every batch is sampled and tested for tenacity, evenness and oil pickup; third-party testing by SGS / BV is supported.

Flexible MOQ

Mixed container loading across varieties, sample-friendly trial orders, and support for new product development.

Experienced in export

Familiar with FOB / CIF terms and full export documentation, serving customers in over 30 countries and regions.

Our Factory
